Based on this contract, you cannot cancel online. Clause 2 states explicitly that online cancellation is not accepted under any circumstances, and clause 4 confirms that attempts to cancel by phone or email will not be processed and billing will continue. Your only valid options are to cancel in person at the club reception or by certified mail. Whichever method you use, your cancellation must be received at least 30 days before your renewal date under clause 3, so act as early as possible and keep proof of delivery.
